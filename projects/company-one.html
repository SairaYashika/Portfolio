Design System, Accessibility

Turning a patchwork of one-off components into a shared, accessible design system the whole product team could build on.

Role
Lead designer
Team
2 designers, 6 engineers
Timeline
8 months, 2026
Platform
Web & iOS

The problem

Product teams were rebuilding the same components in slightly different ways. Interfaces drifted apart, accessibility issues kept resurfacing, and every redesign took longer than it should. [Add the evidence: audit findings, number of duplicated components, support tickets.]

Process

I audited the existing product, grouped components into a small set of patterns, and defined tokens for colour, type and spacing. Each component was built with accessibility requirements first, tested with assistive technology, and documented with usage guidance. [Describe what you tried and dropped.]

Outcome

The system shipped across [N] surfaces, cut design-to-build time by [X%], and brought the product to [WCAG level] compliance. [Add a line about what you learned.]
